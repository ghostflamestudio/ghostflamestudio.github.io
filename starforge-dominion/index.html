The dominion loop

Survive. Adapt. Ascend.

Starforge Dominion is built around pressure that keeps rising and decisions that keep mattering. A successful build opens the door to greater threats, new choices and deeper progression.

01 / CORE

Choose your chassis

Distinct Core chassis including Dominion, Bastion, Reactor, Hive and Singularity shape how a run develops and what kind of machine you become.

02 / EVOLVE

Push beyond upgrades

Structures grow through levels, Overdrive and True Evolutions, turning familiar tools into defining pieces of a late-run build.

03 / ROUTE

Choose where the run goes

Routes, systems and draft choices force trade-offs between immediate survival, resources and the long-term shape of your dominion.

04 / ESCALATE

The universe pushes back

Dominion Heat, elites, flagships and escalating hostile pressure keep successful builds under constant pressure.

05 / COMMAND

Play at your pace

Combat speed controls let you run the battlefield at 1x, 2x, 4x or 8x while keeping the decisions and consequences intact.

06 / VISUALS

Built from code, lit like a machine

Procedural space, bloom, diffraction, chassis-coloured fields and luminous combat effects give the game its neon mechanical identity without relying on a conventional art pipeline.

Two economies, two jobs

Every run feeds the next

Run decisions stay meaningful because temporary power and permanent progression are deliberately separated.

Run currency

Salvage

Earn Salvage during a run through combat, Mining Worlds, rewards and recycling. Spend it on rerolls and decisions that help the current dominion survive.

Permanent progression

Alloy

Convert successful runs into Alloy and invest it into the Forge and Galactic Projects, carrying meaningful progression beyond a single attempt.

Threat-aware combat

Weapons prioritise danger rather than blindly firing at whatever happens to be closest.

Long-run strategy

Chassis, projects, routes, evolutions and heat interact to create builds that change across the length of a run.

Readable at speed

Sharp native-resolution UI and strong controller focus keep the battlefield legible as combat intensity rises.
